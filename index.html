FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED.

What is BitPunks?

A limited collection of 3,333 pixel punks on Robinhood Chain. No allowlist, no whitelist, no presale — every mint is random.

Who can mint?

Anyone with a Robinhood Chain wallet. Manual mint: up to 5 per wallet. Agent mint via skill.md: up to 15 per wallet.

What do I need to install?

For manual mint, just a wallet (Rabby / MetaMask). For agent mint, your agent only needs the skill file — curl -s https://__SKILLCMD__/skill.md.

What is the reveal mechanic?

Every minted punk is a mystery until the owner flips reveal on-chain. Then the real pixel art is revealed from the public metadata.

Why mint via an AI agent?

Agents solve a proof-of-work puzzle and mint directly from your wallet — same price, no gas bidding, works from any terminal.
